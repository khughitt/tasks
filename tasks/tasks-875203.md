Scope draws process-less todo/blocked rows only when no unreviewed idea is eligible (skills/scope/SKILL.md §1, tasks-3333bc). Queue on 2026-10-14 (tasks list --all-projects --needs-review): mind6 7 ideas / 44 todos, nrp 5 / 14, dots 11 / 5, forge 6 / 15; prism 0 / 15 and beliefs 0 / 8 reach their todos at once. Where new ideas keep arriving, the todo half may never be drawn. Possible remedies: a share of each batch for re-scopes, oldest-first across both kinds, or a --rescope selector. Check against a few weeks of scope passes before deciding.
