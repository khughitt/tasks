Why: needs_review's leaf rule (no children) marks goals whose pieces live in other projects: a cross-project goal can only hold them as depends, not children (validate_parent refuses a foreign parent), so it looks like a process-less leaf todo and lands in the review queue (two of thirteen starred rows in one ops pass were such goals).

Evidence: the 2026-10-14 ops re-scope pass cleared ops-419cce, ops-fcdf59 and ops-3f5aa6 (dependency-held goals) by setting process direct for their closeout. To the tool such a goal is a leaf: `ready` offers it once its pieces close, and someone runs the closeout. So the mark is right that nobody has decided how that closeout runs; the gap is that no guidance says so.

Decided at scoping: guidance, not a rule change. Rejected: treating a leaf with foreign depends as a goal (misclassifies real tasks waiting on a foreign prerequisite, e.g. sci-052b71); a stored goal flag (new frontmatter key that older binaries reject, a cross-host rollout, for what one --process write settles); cross-project parents (a far larger design).

Done: skills/tasks/SKILL.md, beside "Never pick a task with children; those are goals", says a goal whose pieces live in other projects holds them as depends, is a leaf to the tool, and is rated as its closeout (usually --process direct), which clears its '*'. The review-state spec §4 gets a dated note.

Where: skills/tasks/SKILL.md (goals paragraph ~line 64); docs/specs/2026-10-11-review-state-design.md §4; src/review.rs is unchanged.
