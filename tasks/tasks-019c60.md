A bounded pass over a cluster of related ideas that leaves each one with a supported next action, a precise question for the user, or a recorded reason to leave it out of active work. Sits between quick-add (capture) and brainstorming (full design); distinct from curate, which creates nothing and changes no status. Two pieces: a shelved status so 'not now' has a home that is neither dropped nor in sight, and the scope skill that runs the pass.
